The "nihilistic penguin" meme comes from a real clip: an Adélie penguin that leaves its colony and heads toward mountains far inland, filmed for Werner Herzog's 2007 documentary Encounters at the End of the World, where Herzog narrates that it is heading toward its death. The clip went viral again as a meme in January 2026. The penguin in this Reel is an original cartoon, and the group-chat skit is fiction.
