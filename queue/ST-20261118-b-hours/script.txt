In this town, you can lend your hours to strangers.
Noah woke up with twenty four.
A girl was late for her exam. He sprinted beside her and gave her six.
A hiker was lost in the dark forest. Noah gave him eight.
A woman was crying on a rooftop. He sat beside her and gave her eight more.
Two hours left. Noah walked to the beach and sat down, tired.
The sun came up. The girl was there. She gave him an hour.
The hiker came running. He gave one too.
The woman hugged him and gave him six.
Noah stood up, glowing.
Every hour he gave away had found its way back.
Who could use one of yours today?
