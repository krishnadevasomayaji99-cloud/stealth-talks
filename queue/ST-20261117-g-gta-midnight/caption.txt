Some GTA 6 players may get to start before their own midnight. Rockstar's support page says Grand Theft Auto VI releases at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions (as quoted by Forbes, 12 Nov). It launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with pre-load from November 10. The catch, as Forbes reports it: US stores treat the country as one region on Eastern time, so players further west can see the game unlock the evening before.
