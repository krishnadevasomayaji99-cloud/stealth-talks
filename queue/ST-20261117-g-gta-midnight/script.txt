Some GTA 6 players get to start before their own midnight.
Rockstar's support page says it releases at midnight local time on November nineteenth, in all regions.
It is coming to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
Pre-load opened on November tenth, so the file can already be waiting on your console.
Now the catch, as Forbes reports it.
US stores treat the whole country as one region.
The clock they follow is Eastern time.
So on the west coast, the game can unlock around nine tonight.
Your calendar and your console clock might not agree.
Do not trust a random post with a launch hour.
Check your own store page before you stay up.
