Outside CIA headquarters there is a message nobody could read.
Four coded messages on one sculpture. Pure nonsense letters.
In late nineteen ninety two, an NSA team cracked three of them.
In nineteen ninety eight, a CIA analyst solved the same three with pencil and paper.
The first public announcement came in nineteen ninety nine, from a computer scientist named Jim Gillogly.
The fourth message did not move.
Then a clue was released. Berlin.
Years later, another. Clock.
The sculpture is called Kryptos. Dedicated November third, nineteen ninety.
Three messages solved. The fourth kept codebreakers guessing for decades.
