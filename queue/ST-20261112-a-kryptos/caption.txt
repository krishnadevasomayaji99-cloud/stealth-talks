Outside the CIA there is a sculpture with a message nobody could fully read.

Kryptos is a sculpture by the American artist Jim Sanborn on the grounds of CIA headquarters in Langley, Virginia, dedicated on 3 November 1990. It carries four encrypted messages. The first three were solved: an NSA team led by Ken Miller reportedly cracked them in late 1992, CIA analyst David Stein did the same with pencil and paper in 1998, and the first public announcement came in 1999 from computer scientist Jim Gillogly. The fourth message stayed unsolved for decades, with clue words released in 2010 (BERLIN) and 2014 (CLOCK).
