Brain rot is not new. Henry David Thoreau used the term in his 1854 book Walden, which Oxford University Press says is the first recorded use. He used it while criticising society's pull toward simpler, duller thinking. In 2024 Oxford University Press named "brain rot" its Word of the Year, and said its use grew 230% between 2023 and 2024.
