In this town, every time you blink, a house disappears.
Mia blinked. Her kitchen vanished.
Everyone froze. Eyes watering. Nobody dared.
Jake sneezed, and blinked. The bakery was gone.
Twenty houses left.
The baker cried. His bread was still inside.
The mayor never blinked. He had no eyelids.
He smiled. His house stood perfectly still.
Even the sun looked away, so it would not blink.
Then the mayor looked at you and whispered, your turn.
You blinked.
The last house was yours.
