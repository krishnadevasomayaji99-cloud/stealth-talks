In this town, everyone gets ten words a day.
Leo woke up angry. He spent five on his dad. Shut up, leave me alone.
He spent three on his boss. Not my problem.
Two words left.
His phone rang. It was mum. He let it ring.
Then he found his sister on the roof, crying. She had spent all ten before noon.
Leo sat beside her. He hugged her and whispered, I'm here.
Zero words left. She smiled.
The whole city went quiet, as if it was listening.
Now count your words today. How many were kind?
