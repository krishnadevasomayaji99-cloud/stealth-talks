In this town, everyone gets ten words a day. How many of yours were kind?

Real world: a University of Texas at Austin study published in Science found that both women and men averaged about 16,000 words per day, so ten would be gone before breakfast. Animated fiction with original characters; the town, Leo and his family are not real.
