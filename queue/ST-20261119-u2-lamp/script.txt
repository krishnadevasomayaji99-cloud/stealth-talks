Grandma Rosa's lamp was almost dark.

He kept running until he reached the beach house.
Rosa was waiting alone in her chair.
Eli said, I'm here, Grandma.
The lamp flared, and one visit became nine as the neighbors arrived.
Rosa hugged him and cried.
