In 1932, the Australian army went to war with emus. The emus won.

In November 1932, soldiers went to Campion, Western Australia, after farmers who were ex-servicemen asked Defence Minister George Pearce for help with roughly 20,000 emus eating their wheat. Major G.P.W. Meredith led the operation with two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds. By 8 November about 2,500 rounds had been fired; estimates of emus killed in that first phase range from about 50 to several hundred, and Meredith reported no casualties among his men. The first mission was withdrawn on 8 November. Troops went back on 13 November and were recalled in December. A bounty system later proved effective.
