A school was shut down because nobody could stop making one sound.
It began on the thirtieth of January, at a girls' boarding school in Kashasha, near Lake Victoria.
Three girls started, and could not stop.
Within weeks, ninety five of one hundred fifty nine pupils were affected.
The school closed in March.
The girls went home, and it followed them to nearby villages and another school.
Attacks lasted anywhere from hours to sixteen days.
Doctors tested food and toxins. They found nothing.
The sound was laughing. Real, uncontrollable laughing.
Researchers class it as mass psychogenic illness.
