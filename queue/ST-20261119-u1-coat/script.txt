In this town, your coat gets warmer each time you give it away.
Mia's coat had been ice cold all winter.
At the bus stop, a boy was shivering.
Mia hesitated. Then she handed him her coat.
Warm light spread over him, and the counter jumped.
She ran across the roofs to the old baker, shivering.
She gave him her scarf, and he smiled and cried.
Back home, her room was freezing.
Then someone knocked. The boy had brought her a glowing coat.
Mia hugged him and finally stopped shivering.
Somebody near you is cold today. Give them something.
