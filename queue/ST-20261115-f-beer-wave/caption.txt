17 October 1814, St Giles, London: a giant wooden vat at Meux's brewery burst at about 5.30 pm. The wave brought down a brewery wall and a neighbouring house, and eight people died, including four-year-old Hannah Banfield. Two days later the coroner's jury found they died "casually, accidentally, and by misfortune". Sources differ on the details: one account gives the vat's contents as about a million pints, another as 3,555 barrels, and they differ on the exact street and on how some victims died.
