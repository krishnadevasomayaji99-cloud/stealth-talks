A narrow street, in the summer heat, with no music anywhere.
A woman steps into it and cannot stop moving.
No music. No reason. One day goes by. Then another.
Within days, dozens of strangers have joined her. By August, hundreds. They dance for days, with barely any rest.
The doctors rule out the stars. They blame overheated blood.
So the city makes a choice. It opens the guildhalls.
Then it hires musicians.
Nobody has ever agreed on what caused it. The city was Strasbourg. This was over five hundred years ago.
