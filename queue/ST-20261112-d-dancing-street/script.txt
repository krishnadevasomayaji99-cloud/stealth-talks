A woman steps into a street and cannot stop moving.
No music. No reason. One day goes by. Then another.
Within a month, hundreds of strangers have joined her. They dance for days, with barely any rest. Accounts say some collapse from exhaustion.
The doctors rule out the stars. They blame hot blood.
So the city makes a choice. It opens the guildhalls and builds a wooden stage.
Then it hires musicians.
Nobody has ever agreed on what caused it. The city was Strasbourg. This was over five hundred years ago.
