Summer 1518, Strasbourg: a woman starts dancing in the street and does not stop. According to one history article, more people joined within days and a month later there were supposedly 400 dancers. The authorities opened two guildhalls and a grain market, built a wooden stage and hired musicians, hoping the dancers would tire themselves out. It did not work well. Nobody knows for sure what caused it; mass hysteria and ergot poisoning are the leading theories and both are debated. The 400 figure and the death toll are reported loosely, so treat them as approximate.
