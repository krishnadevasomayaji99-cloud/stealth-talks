Summer 1518. One woman is dancing in a Strasbourg street, and she cannot stop.
Her name is Frau Troffea.
Within a few days, more people join her.
A month in, there are supposedly four hundred dancers.
Nobody knows why it started, and nobody can make it stop.
So the authorities try something strange.
They open two guildhalls and a grain market, and build a wooden stage.
Then they hire musicians, hoping the dancers will tire themselves out.
It did not work well.
Historians still argue about the cause, and the tape ends mid-step.
