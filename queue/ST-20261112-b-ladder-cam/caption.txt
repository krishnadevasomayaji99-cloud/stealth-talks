Who keeps moving the ladder? A found-footage mystery with a very small culprit.

Goats are naturally curious, agile and able to climb and balance in precarious places, and some goats will even climb trees. They are also notorious for escaping pens by testing fences and enclosures. The farmer, the camera and the goat in this story are fictional.
