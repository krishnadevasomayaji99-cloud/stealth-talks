Something moves the ladder every night. Nobody has seen who.
Monday, the ladder is by the barn. Tuesday, it leans on the wall. Wednesday, it is on the roof.
Each night the ladder goes a little higher. Each morning the farmer checks the knots on the barn door. It does not help.
So the farmer sets a camera. Three in the morning. The footage shakes.
A shape steps into the frame. Small. Patient. Not afraid at all.
It looks straight into the lens. It is a goat.
Goats are famously agile climbers, and some even climb trees. To a goat, a ladder is just a lazy tree.
Thursday. The camera is on the roof.
