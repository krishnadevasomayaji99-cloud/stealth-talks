The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s, when he was a university student. He timed his study sessions with a kitchen timer shaped like a tomato, and "pomodoro" is the Italian word for tomato. The classic rhythm is 25 minutes of focused work followed by a short break of about five minutes. The student, the desk and the smiling timer in this story are fictional.
