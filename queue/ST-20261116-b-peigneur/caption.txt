On 20 November 1980, Lake Peigneur in Louisiana, about 10 feet deep, drained into a salt mine beneath it. UPI reports that the mine's owner alleged a Texaco drilling rig had punctured the roof; this was a claim and some experts disputed it. IFLScience reports the rig, barges and part of an island were swallowed, the Delcambre Canal ran backwards, nobody died, and the lake refilled in nearly two days, now far deeper. UPI reports a settlement of about 32 million dollars. Sources differ on the barge count, so none is given.
