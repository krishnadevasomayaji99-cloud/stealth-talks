In this town, every thought you think plays on the loudspeaker.
Mia woke up and thought, I hate Mondays.
The loudspeaker said it for her. Everyone nodded.
Then she thought, the mayor looks strange.
The loudspeaker said that too. The mayor froze.
He panicked and thought, please do not think about my secret.
Now the whole town thought about his secret.
The loudspeaker got very loud.
It announced the mayor is not one man.
He is three raccoons in a coat.
The raccoons waved.
Mia thought, I knew it.
Then the loudspeaker whispered, who is thinking this?
