A Louisiana lake was ten feet deep, and then it drained.
On a November Thursday, a drilling rig was working on the water.
Then the whole lake began to swirl.
It swallowed the rig, several barges, trees and part of an island.
A canal even ran backwards to fill the hole.
About fifty people below the lake got out, and nobody died.
Within about two days the lake had refilled, far deeper than before.
Underneath was a salt mine, and the drilling had allegedly broken into it.
The mine's owner later settled for thirty two million dollars.
