A bridge was open for only four months, and it ended with one unlikely casualty.
The Tacoma Narrows Bridge in Washington State opened on 1 July 1940 and was nicknamed Galloping Gertie because its deck moved in the wind. On 7 November 1940, in winds of roughly 40 mph, the main span twisted and fell at around 11 a.m. A spaniel named Tubby, left in a car on the bridge, was reported as the only fatality. The collapse changed bridge design, and models are now wind-tunnel tested. Sources differ on exact wind speed and on Tubby's details.
