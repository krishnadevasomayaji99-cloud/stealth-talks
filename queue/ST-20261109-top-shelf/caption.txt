Pepper is not the first cat to climb back from a bad day.

In 1799 a cat named Trim was born at sea aboard HMS Reliance. He later sailed with Matthew Flinders on HMS Investigator during the circumnavigation of Australia (1801 to 1803). One account says Trim once fell overboard, swam back to the ship and climbed a rope to get back on board. When Flinders was detained on Mauritius, Trim went missing and was presumed dead; Flinders wrote him a tribute. A bronze statue of Trim sits outside the Mitchell Library in Sydney (unveiled 28 March 1996).
