Sweden's greatest warship made it less than a mile. The strangest part is what happened the day before.
In 1628 the warship Vasa, 220 feet long with 64 guns, sailed from Stockholm and sank within about 25 minutes, taking around 30 lives with it. The day before, 30 men ran across the deck to test how it rolled. The inquest blamed the design: a thicker deck and heavier guns on the upper deck made it top-heavy. The shipbuilder had died the year before. Raised in 1961, roughly 95 percent of the wood was intact.
