A ship was found at sea with six months of food on board and nobody to eat it.
The brigantine Mary Celeste left New York for Genoa on 7 November 1872 with Captain Benjamin Briggs, his wife and their two year old daughter. The Dei Gratia found her on 4 December 1872. The cargo of about 1,700 barrels of alcohol was in the hold, with provisions for six months and plenty of drinking water. The lifeboat and the navigation instruments were gone, while the log book was still aboard. The last log entry was nine days and about 500 miles away. A court in Gibraltar met in December 1872 and found no answers, and the reason for the abandonment has never been determined.
