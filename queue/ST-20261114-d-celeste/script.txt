A ship was found at sea with nobody on board.
Her name was Mary Celeste. She had left New York in November, bound for Genoa.
On deck there was no sign of the captain, his wife, or their two year old daughter.
There was six months of food, and plenty of drinking water.
The cargo, seventeen hundred barrels of alcohol, was still in the hold.
The log book was still aboard. The lifeboat and the navigation instruments were gone.
The last log entry was nine days and five hundred miles away.
A court in Gibraltar looked into it and had no answers.
Six months of food, and nobody ever came back for it.
