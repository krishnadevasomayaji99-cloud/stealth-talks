In this town, a question mark floats over you when someone worries.
Mia walked into school with one above her head.
Her teacher asked if she was okay. Mia smiled and said fine.
At home, her dad stared at the marks and a second one appeared.
He asked again, softly, and a third one climbed up.
Mia ran outside and fell into the snow.
Her best friend caught up, shivering, with a mark of her own.
Mia finally said, I'm not okay.
The marks burst into light and floated away.
Her friend hugged her and cried.
Someone asked if you're okay. Tell them the truth.
