Sixteen million people pre-ordered the same game.
Jay was one of them, and he stared at the clock all night.
Every minute felt like an hour, and his hands shook.
At midnight the download bar crawled and Jay paced.
He refreshed the page forty times and ate cold noodles.
By morning he ran to the roof to watch the sunrise and sulk.
Then his little sister asked to hold the controller.
He wanted to say no, because it was his first.
Jay hesitated, then handed it over.
She pressed start and giggled until she snorted.
The review score said ninety nine, but her smile said more.
