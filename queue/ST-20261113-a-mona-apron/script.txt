A painting vanished from the Louvre, and nobody noticed for a day.
Sunday night, a man slipped into a storage closet and waited.
At dawn he walked out, lifted the painting off the wall, and carried it to a stairwell.
But the courtyard door was locked. He was trapped.
A plumber saw him fighting the doorknob and opened it for him. He thought the man was a coworker.
Why? Because the thief was wearing a white apron, just like the museum staff.
Vincenzo Peruggia, a former Louvre handyman, was arrested in Florence two years later.
Tuesday at noon, a visiting artist finally asked where it was.
