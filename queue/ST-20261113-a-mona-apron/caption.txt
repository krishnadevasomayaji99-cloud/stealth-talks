A painting vanished from the world's biggest museum and nobody noticed for more than a day. Vincenzo Peruggia, a former Louvre handyman, hid in a storage closet on the evening of 20 August 1911, then walked out the next morning in a white apron like the museum's staff. He took the painting from its frame in a stairwell, and a plumber helped him open a locked courtyard door, thinking he was a trapped coworker. Staff did not realise it was missing until around noon on Tuesday, when a visiting artist asked a guard to find it. Peruggia was arrested in Florence in December 1913 after trying to sell it, and the painting returned to the Louvre in January 1914. The characters in this animation are fictional.
