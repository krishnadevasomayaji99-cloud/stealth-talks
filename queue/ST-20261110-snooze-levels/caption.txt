Level four snoozer? There is a reason it is nine minutes.

One popular explanation, per Mental Floss: snooze buttons arrived in the 1950s, alarm clock gears were already standardised, so engineers could pick a snooze slightly over nine minutes or slightly over ten. They chose nine because early reports suggested ten minutes let people drift back into deep sleep. The Straight Dope calls its own related explanation a theory, so treat the exact history as unsettled. The skit is fictional.
