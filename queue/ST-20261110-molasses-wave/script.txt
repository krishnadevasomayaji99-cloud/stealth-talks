Boston. January nineteen nineteen. A calm afternoon in the North End.
Suddenly the ground rumbles. A wave rushes down the street. Thirty five miles an hour. Estimates say up to twenty five feet tall.
Plot twist. The wave is not water. It is molasses.
Two point three million gallons. It really happened. Twenty one people died, and about a hundred and fifty were hurt.
Even the cleanup was wild. A boat sprayed salt water on the streets. The harbour stayed brown until summer.
The company blamed anarchists. A court disagreed. They paid six hundred and twenty eight thousand dollars.
