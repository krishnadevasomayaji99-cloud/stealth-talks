Telegraph machines that sparked on their own, operators who got shocked, and a line that worked with the batteries off. This is the Carrington Event, the great solar storm of early September 1859. Space.com and NOAA report aurora seen far south, shocks and sparks for operators, and small fires in some offices. A NASA-hosted paper of eyewitness reports says a Boston-Portland line ran about two hours on auroral current alone. Sources differ on exact dates within the storm run and on which places saw aurora, so details here are kept general.
