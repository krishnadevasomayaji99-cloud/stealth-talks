Mo's first time in a fancy cafe. Level three: still on the table.

A real bit of cafe history behind the skit: according to Wikipedia, the first coffeehouse in London opened in 1652 (founded by Pasqua Rosée), and early London coffeehouses were nicknamed "penny universities" because entry could sometimes cost one penny and gave access to books and print news. The seating mix-up is fiction, and Mo is an invented character.
