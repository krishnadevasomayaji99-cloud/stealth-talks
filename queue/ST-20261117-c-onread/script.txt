In this town, every message you leave on read becomes a ghost.
Mia left one on read. A small ghost appeared on her porch.
She left two. Two ghosts sat down beside it.
They never spoke. They only waited.
Jake ignored nine texts from his grandma. Nine ghosts filled his yard.
The mayor called a town meeting about the ghosts.
Nobody came, so the mayor got a ghost too.
Then Mia typed back. Sorry, I was busy.
The first ghost smiled, and faded. It had waited so long.
Every other ghost in town turned around.
They were all looking at your porch.
Your phone buzzed. Twenty three unread.
