In this town, a flower grows over your head every time someone says thank you.
Grandpa Leo's flower was almost gone.
He fixed bikes and shared lunches, and nobody ever thanked him.
His grandson Sam noticed.
Sam ran into the forest to find the kids Leo had helped.
He fell in the mud and kept running.
At the beach, he found the girl whose bike Leo fixed.
She laughed and said thank you out loud.
One flower popped up. Then ten more.
Sam raced home. The room filled with petals.
Leo hugged him and cried.
Somebody in your life never hears thank you. Tell them today.
