In this town, a flower grows over your head every time someone says thank you, and Grandpa Leo's was almost gone. The people who help quietly are the ones we forget to thank. Think of one person who never hears it, and tell them today, out loud. Animated fiction with original characters; the town, the flowers and the people are not real.
