In this town, a bar over your head shows how much of your day you wasted.
Leo woke up at seven and his bar already said ten percent.
He scrolled for one minute. Twenty percent.
He scrolled through a video about being productive. Forty percent.
At school every other bar sat at zero, and Leo's kept climbing.
His teacher asked what he did all morning. Leo said nothing, and sixty percent glowed above him.
He ran up to the roof at sunrise, and the bar hit eighty.
Up there nobody was looking at anything but the sky.
Leo finally noticed that everyone's bars were empty because their hands were empty.
He slid his hands into his pocket and the number started dropping.
By the beach it was zero, and he finally saw the waves.
Look at your bar right now. It just moved.
