Quick story. A railroad foreman named Phineas Gage had the worst day at work. Ever.
Eighteen forty eight. Vermont. He is packing blasting powder with a metal rod. Three feet seven inches long. Thirteen pounds.
The powder explodes. The rod goes in through his cheek. Behind his left eye. And out of the top of his head.
Phineas sits up. Talks. Walks. Within minutes.
Plot twist. He lived about eleven more years.
The internet says he became a different man. Plot twist two. Historians say that story is exaggerated.
Moral. Your head is not empty. And the story you heard might be wrong. Send this to someone with no thoughts.
