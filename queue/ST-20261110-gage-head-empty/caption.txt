No thoughts, head empty? Phineas Gage would like a word.

Phineas Gage was a railroad construction foreman in Vermont. On 13 September 1848 a blasting accident drove a tamping iron, 3 ft 7 in long and 13 1/4 lb, through the left side of his face and out of the top of his skull. He spoke within minutes, walked with little assistance, and died in 1860, about 11 to 12 years later. The dramatic personality-change story is disputed; Wikipedia notes accounts have been described as severely exaggerated and distorted.
