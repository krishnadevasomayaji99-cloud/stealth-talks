In November 1932, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight emus. This is real.
Farmers in Western Australia said around twenty thousand emus were eating their wheat. So the army turned up with two machine guns and ten thousand bullets.
In the first week the soldiers fired about twenty five hundred rounds. Estimates of how many emus they got range from fifty to several hundred.
No soldiers were hurt. The emus were not stopped. The first mission ended within a week.
Plot twist. What worked better was a bounty, not the army.
The emus won, and they have never said thank you.
