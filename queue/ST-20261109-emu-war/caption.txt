In 1932, the Australian army went to war with emus. The emus won.

In November 1932 troops arrived at Campion, Western Australia, after wheat farmers petitioned the Defence Minister for help against a reported population of at least 20,000 emus. Major G.P.W. Meredith led the Seventh Heavy Battery of the Royal Australian Artillery with two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds. About 2,500 rounds were fired, killing an estimated 50 to 200 emus (estimates vary). No soldiers were hurt, the birds were not stopped, and the troops were recalled within a week. A bounty later proved more effective.
