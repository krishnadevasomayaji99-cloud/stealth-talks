A dead man was put on trial, and he was in the room.
Pope Stephen the sixth had his predecessor, Formosus, dug up.
The body was dressed in papal robes and propped up on the throne.
A deacon was appointed to speak for the corpse.
The charge was usurping the papacy. The verdict was guilty.
Every act of his reign was declared null and void.
Three fingers, the ones used for blessing, were cut off, and the body was thrown into the Tiber.
Then the judge's luck ended. Within months, Stephen was imprisoned and strangled.
And the dead pope? Pulled from the river and returned to his tomb.
