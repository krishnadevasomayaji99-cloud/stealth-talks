One night in August, a radio telescope in Ohio listened to the sky.
It listened to one patch for seventy two seconds.
Nobody was watching. The printout sat in a pile.
Days later, an astronomer found six strange characters in the data.
The signal was far stronger than the background hiss.
He circled them. Then he pointed the telescope back at the same spot, again and again.
Nothing came back. The sky stayed silent.
So he picked up his pen and wrote one word beside the circle: Wow.
The signal has never repeated, and nobody has proved what made it.
Seventy two seconds. Still no answer.
