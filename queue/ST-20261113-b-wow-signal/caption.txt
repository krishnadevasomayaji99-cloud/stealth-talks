A radio telescope in Ohio recorded a signal for exactly 72 seconds, and it has never been heard again. On 15 August 1977, Ohio State University's Big Ear telescope picked up a narrowband burst from the direction of Sagittarius. Days later, volunteer astronomer Jerry Ehman spotted it in the data printout, circled the readings, and wrote "Wow!" beside them. The characters 6EQUJ5 are not a message but a record of the signal's strength over time. Later searches found nothing, and its source remains unconfirmed. This animation uses fictional visuals.
