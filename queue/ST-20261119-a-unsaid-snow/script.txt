In this town, the words you never say turn into snow.
Walt never told his son one thing. He wanted to. The words just would not come out. By Monday, it was ten centimetres deep.
By Friday, it reached his windows.
He sat inside and stared at the phone, too stubborn to call.
His son knocked anyway. Walt opened the door, and snow poured in.
He whispered, I'm proud of you. His voice shook. His son held on tighter.
The snow stopped. The sky turned gold. Everything melted at once.
Say it before it snows.
