Breaking news. The Eiffel Tower is growing. Paris is panicking.
On hot days it stretches up to fifteen centimetres. Scientists are baffled.
Plot twist. They are not baffled. It is just iron getting warm.
Seven thousand three hundred tonnes of iron. All solids expand when they heat up.
It even leans a tiny bit away from the sun. Then it straightens up at night. In winter it shrinks back down.
So no, the tower is not alive. It is just very dramatic about summer.
