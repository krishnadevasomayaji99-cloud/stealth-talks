There was one biscuit left, and two of them wanted it.
Maya said she would split it fairly. Her cat, Pepper, said nothing, which is what cats say when they are planning.
Maya broke the biscuit. One half was huge and plain. The other was a tiny crumb, holding every chocolate chip.
Maya grabbed the big half. Pepper took the crumb, and looked her dead in the eye.
Maya chewed plain dough for ages. Pepper had planned this since breakfast.
