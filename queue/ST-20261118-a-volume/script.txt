In this town, your voice has a volume bar over your head.
Mia's bar was almost empty.
At school, she whispered hello. Nobody heard.
She dropped her books. Nobody helped.
At home, she told her mum she was fine. The bar did not move.
Then her pet raccoon ran out into the snow.
Mia ran after him. She slipped on the ice.
The pond cracked. The raccoon was stuck in the middle.
Mia took a breath and screamed his name.
The bar filled up. The whole town heard her.
Her mum came running and hugged her.
Somebody near you has an empty bar today. Ask how they really are.
