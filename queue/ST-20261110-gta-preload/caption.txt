GTA 6 preloading starts today. Make some room.

Per Rockstar, as reported by VGC on 3 November 2026: the pre-load begins on 10 November and can be up to 212 GB, depending on the device. Xbox Series X/S pre-load opens at 12am GMT on 10 November; on PS5 it starts at midnight local time. The game is set for 19 November. The skit is fictional.
