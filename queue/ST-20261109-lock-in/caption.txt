Locking in is easy. Staying locked in is the plot twist.

The Pomodoro Technique was developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. It is named after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer he used as a university student. Work intervals are typically 25 minutes, followed by a short break of typically 5 to 10 minutes.
