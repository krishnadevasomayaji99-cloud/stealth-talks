Okay so there was one biscuit left. One. And two of them wanted it.
Maya goes, I'll split it fair. Pepper the cat says nothing, because cats don't talk, they plot.
Crack. One half is huge and plain. The other is a tiny crumb, packed with chocolate chips.
Maya grabs the big half like she just won. Pepper takes the crumb and looks her dead in the eye.
Maya bites. Plain dough. Pepper bites. Chocolate. Pepper wins.
Except, plot twist. Maya's plain half? Solid chocolate inside. And Pepper's chips? Raisins. Pepper had been had.
She planned this since breakfast. The biscuit planned it since the factory.
