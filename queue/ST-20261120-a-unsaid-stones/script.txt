In this town, every thank you you don't say becomes a stone.
Leo woke up with one on his shoulder.
It was small, and he told himself it was nothing.
In the forest his coach waved, and Leo looked away. A second stone dropped.
Leo had trained with him for three years and never once spoke up.
At home his mom left a lunch note. He said nothing. Third.
The stones clinked as he walked, and every step felt heavier.
Four stones dragged him down onto the sand.
He lay there and finally understood what they were.
Then he stood up and shouted, thank you.
His voice cracked, and it felt like setting down a backpack.
The stones burst into birds and flew away.
His coach laughed and hugged him.
Say the thank you today.
