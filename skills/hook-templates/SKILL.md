# Hook templates (Krishna, 10 Nov 2026: "save it and use it")
Original idea list was for talking-head content. We are faceless/animated, so keep the hook line and swap the film direction for an animated scene with an invented character, sound stinger and on-screen text.

1. **"Stop doing this if you want [X]."** Hook = the mistake, in the first 1 s. Scene: character does the mistake, comic punishment escalates, twist fix. Caption: sourced true fact on the topic.
2. **"I tried this for 7 days and the results shocked me."** Scene: day counter (Day 1..7) montage, results get absurd, final-day twist. Fits the progressive-levels recipe in the ViralSpy recipe book. Never claim real results as fact; it is a fictional character.
3. **"The secret they don't tell you about [topic]."** Scene: myth-busting reveal with a sourced true fact (this is the dark-history/caption-fact slot). Never imply a secret scandal about a real person or company.

Rules: at least one of the day's 4 Reels uses one of these hooks; rotate, never the same hook twice in a row; log which was used in playbook.md and compare watch time/shares next day. Pair with the brainrot/relatable direction in daily-loop.
